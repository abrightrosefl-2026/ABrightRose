Founding Users Beta — Terms & Privacy Notice

Draft for review. This is a plain-language working draft prepared for A Bright Rose Cleaning Services. Have an attorney review and finalize it before the public launch of the beta.

1. What the beta is

The Founding Users Beta lets selected cleaning business owners and managers test the ABR Field App for a limited time (currently 60 days from approval). Access is free and no credit card is required. The beta is a pre-release product: features may change, break or be removed, and we may extend, shorten or end access at any time.

2. Your sandbox workspace

Beta testers use a private demo workspace with sample data. It is separate from A Bright Rose Cleaning Services' own client, crew and financial records, which are not visible to beta testers. Please do not enter confidential customer information, passwords or payment details into the beta.

3. Future paid plans

A paid subscription may be offered after the beta. Joining it is optional. We may offer founding members a discount; its terms are not set and nothing in this notice promises a particular price, discount or duration.

4. What we collect

When you apply: your name, email, phone (optional), company, role, team size, number of sites, facility types, current tools, your description of your challenges, and how you heard about the beta. While you test: which screens and buttons you use, when you visit, and the feedback and ratings you submit.

5. How we use it

To review applications, give you access, contact you about the beta, understand how the app is used, improve the product, and decide whether and how to offer a commercial version. If you tick the permission box on the feedback form, we may quote your feedback publicly with your name and company; otherwise we will not.

6. Sharing and security

We do not sell your information. It is stored with our service providers (for example our database and hosting providers) and protected with access controls. Only authorized A Bright Rose managers can view beta data.

7. Your choices

You may ask us to access or delete your beta data at any time by calling (561) 202-7885 or replying to any beta email. We will delete your application, account and usage data on request.

8. Feedback

You are free to share feedback and ideas. By doing so, you allow us to use them to improve our products without obligation to you.

9. No warranty

The beta is provided "as is" without warranties of any kind, to the extent permitted by law.
