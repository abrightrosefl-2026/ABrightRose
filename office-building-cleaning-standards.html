Office Building Cleaning Standards: What's Normal?

Published by the ABR team · Updated October 2026

Key takeaways
  • Normal service is nightly or several times a week for restrooms, trash, floors and common areas, with periodic detail work.
  • In multi-tenant buildings, know which areas the landlord cleans and which you do.
  • Your scope and checklist define the standard you can enforce.
Published by A Bright Rose Cleaning Services, a commercial-only cleaning company in West Palm Beach, FL serving Palm Beach County.

Office buildings tend to follow a recognizable pattern. Knowing it helps you judge what you are getting.

Common areas (lobbies, hallways, elevators)

  • Floors cleaned daily, with entry mats checked
  • Glass doors and elevator interiors wiped daily
  • High-touch points disinfected daily
  • Dusting and detail work weekly or monthly

Restrooms

  • Cleaned and restocked every service day
  • Fixtures disinfected and floors mopped with the correct dilution
  • Deep detail on grout, partitions and drains weekly or monthly

See restroom cleaning standards.

Tenant spaces and offices

  • Trash and recycling emptied nightly
  • Vacuuming or mopping several times a week
  • Desks usually left alone unless cleared by the tenant
  • Break rooms cleaned daily

Landlord versus tenant

In multi-tenant buildings, read your lease. The landlord often handles lobbies, common restrooms and exteriors, while the tenant handles the suite. Gaps between the two are a common source of complaints.

What a good routine includes

  • A written checklist specific to the building
  • Color-coded cloths and mops to avoid cross-contamination
  • Scheduled periodic tasks, tracked on a calendar
  • A supervisor who inspects regularly
  • A single contact for requests

How to check the standard

  1. Walk the building on Monday morning.
  2. Look at restrooms, break rooms, entry glass and floor edges.
  3. Compare to the checklist.
  4. Give feedback in writing.
Reminder: "Standard" is what you agree in writing. Ask the vendor for the checklist and keep a copy.
