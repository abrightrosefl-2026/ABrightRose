Checklists & Standards

The complete commercial cleaning checklist: daily, weekly, and monthly

Published by the ABR team · Updated June 2026 · Free to print and use in your facility

A checklist isn't bureaucracy — it's the only way cleaning quality survives a crew change, a sick day, or a busy week. Whether you run your own in-house team or manage an outside vendor, this list is the baseline. Print it, adapt the frequencies to your building, and use it to audit whoever does the work.

How to use this: "Daily" assumes a facility cleaned five or more nights a week. If your building is cleaned less often, shift daily tasks to "every visit" and compress the weekly list accordingly.

Daily tasks

Offices & workstations

  • Empty all trash and recycling; replace liners
  • Wipe and disinfect desk surfaces that are cleared (never move papers)
  • Spot-clean glass on entry doors and partitions
  • Vacuum carpeted traffic lanes; dust-mop hard floors
  • Disinfect shared touch points: door handles, light switches, elevator buttons

Restrooms

  • Clean and disinfect toilets, urinals, and sinks — clean first, then disinfect
  • Refill soap, paper towels, and tissue; never leave a dispenser at less than a quarter full
  • Disinfect stall latches, flush handles, and faucet handles
  • Sweep and wet-mop floors with a restroom-only mop head
  • Empty sanitary disposal units; check floor drains for odor

Break rooms & kitchens

  • Wipe counters, tables, and chair backs
  • Clean exterior of appliances; disinfect microwave handle and fridge handle
  • Empty trash; wet-mop floor
  • Run or empty the dishwasher if part of scope

Weekly tasks

  • Full vacuum of all carpet, edge to edge, including under accessible furniture
  • Dust horizontal surfaces above and below desk height: sills, ledges, baseboards
  • Clean interior glass and partition walls fully
  • Machine-scrub or wet-mop all hard floors, not just traffic lanes
  • Disinfect phones, keyboards (if in scope), and conference room equipment
  • Pour water into floor drains to keep traps from drying out

Monthly tasks

  • High dusting: vents, ceiling corners, light fixtures, tops of cabinets
  • Vacuum upholstered furniture and fabric panels
  • Clean air vent covers and returns
  • Detail baseboards, corners, and door frames
  • Inspect and spot-treat carpet stains; schedule extraction if needed
  • Burnish or buff hard floors per the floor-care program

The part most checklists skip: verification

A checklist that nobody signs is a suggestion. Build in three habits:

  1. Initial and date. Whoever completes the work initials each section, each visit.
  2. Photo-verify the failure points. Restroom dispensers and trash areas account for most complaints. A timestamped photo takes ten seconds.
  3. Score one random area per week. Pick a room, inspect against the list, and record a pass/fail score. Trends matter more than any single night.
