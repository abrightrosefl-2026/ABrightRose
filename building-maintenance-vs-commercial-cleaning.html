Building Maintenance vs. Commercial Cleaning: What's the Difference?

Published by the ABR team · Updated October 2026

Key takeaways
  • Cleaning keeps surfaces and spaces clean. Maintenance keeps the building and its systems working.
  • Some tasks sit in between, so assign each one to a named party in writing.
  • Clear boundaries prevent finger-pointing.
Published by A Bright Rose Cleaning Services, a commercial-only cleaning company in West Palm Beach, FL serving Palm Beach County.

Many building managers use the words interchangeably, but they are different jobs with different people.

Commercial cleaning (janitorial)

Focuses on cleanliness and hygiene:

  • Trash and recycling
  • Restrooms and break rooms
  • Floors and carpets
  • Dusting and surface cleaning
  • Glass and entries
  • Restocking consumables
  • Disinfection of high-touch areas

Building maintenance

Focuses on the physical building and its systems:

  • Plumbing, electrical and HVAC repairs
  • Lighting replacement
  • Doors, locks and hardware
  • Roofing, drainage and exterior repairs
  • Landscaping and parking lots
  • Pest control and fire safety systems
  • Preventive maintenance programs

The gray areas

TaskCommon owner
Changing light bulbsMaintenance, sometimes cleaning if in scope
Replacing paper dispensersMaintenance
Restocking dispensersCleaning
Pressure washingSpecialty contractor or maintenance
Window cleaningSpecialty contractor
Spill cleanupCleaning
Reporting leaks and damageCleaning crew reports, maintenance fixes

How to avoid gaps

  1. List every recurring task in the building.
  2. Assign each task to a named party.
  3. Put cleaning tasks in the cleaning scope and maintenance tasks in the maintenance plan.
  4. Ask the cleaning crew to report problems they see, such as leaks, burnt-out lights and broken fixtures.

Why reporting matters

Cleaning crews are in the building when no one else is. A good crew spots leaks, damage and safety hazards early. Make it easy to report them with a clear contact and process.

Practical tip: Add a line to your scope: "Crew reports maintenance issues to [contact] within one business day."
