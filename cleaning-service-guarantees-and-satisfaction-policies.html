Cleaning Service Guarantees and Satisfaction Policies: What to Look For

Published by the ABR team · Updated October 2026

Key takeaways
  • A guarantee only matters if it is written, specific and easy to use.
  • Look for a re-clean window, a clear reporting method and a defined cure period.
  • Be careful with vague promises such as 'satisfaction guaranteed' with no terms.
Published by A Bright Rose Cleaning Services, a commercial-only cleaning company in West Palm Beach, FL serving Palm Beach County.

Almost every cleaning company says it guarantees its work. The useful question is what the guarantee actually promises.

What a good guarantee includes

  • A re-clean promise. If a task in the agreed scope was missed, the company returns and fixes it at no extra charge, within a stated time such as 24 hours.
  • A clear way to report. A phone number, email or app where you can report an issue, and a named person who responds.
  • A cure period. A set number of days for the company to fix repeated problems before you can end the contract.
  • A link to the scope. The guarantee applies to the written scope, so the scope needs to be specific. See the contract checklist.
  • Documentation. Photos or notes of the issue and the fix.

What to be careful about

  • Promises that are not in the contract.
  • Guarantees that exclude most real problems.
  • Short claim windows that are hard to meet.
  • A guarantee that covers the clean but not damage. Check insurance too, as explained in commercial cleaning insurance.

How to use a guarantee well

  1. Report problems quickly, in writing, with a photo.
  2. Give the company a fair chance to fix it within the agreed time.
  3. Keep a short log of issues and responses.
  4. If problems repeat, follow the process in when commercial cleaning falls short.

Ask before you sign

  • What exactly do you guarantee, and for how long?
  • How do I report a problem, and how fast will you respond?
  • What happens if the same issue returns?
  • Is this in the contract?

A confident company will answer clearly and put it in writing.
