Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Beta access is free for 60 days and no credit card is required. A paid plan may be offered later; signing up for it will always be your choice.

Will you see my real customer data?

Your beta workspace is a sandbox filled with sample data. Please don't enter confidential customer information. We record your answers, feedback and how you use the app so we can improve it.

What's expected of me?

Try the three starter tasks and share honest feedback. A short call is helpful but optional.

Is it ready for my whole business?

It's a beta — things will change and some features may be rough. That's exactly why we want your feedback.
