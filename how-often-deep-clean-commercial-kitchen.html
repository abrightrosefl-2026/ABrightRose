How Often Should You Deep Clean Your Commercial Kitchen?

Published by the ABR team · Updated October 2026

Key takeaways
  • Daily cleaning handles food-contact surfaces. Deep cleaning targets equipment, floors, walls and hidden grease.
  • Exhaust hood and duct cleaning frequency depends on cooking volume under NFPA 96: monthly, quarterly, semiannually or annually.
  • Hood and duct cleaning is specialized work done by trained contractors.
Published by A Bright Rose Cleaning Services, a commercial-only cleaning company in West Palm Beach, FL serving Palm Beach County.

Daily cleaning keeps a kitchen safe to operate. Deep cleaning removes the buildup that daily cleaning misses. The right schedule depends on volume, menu and equipment.

A practical schedule

TaskTypical frequency
Food-contact surfaces, prep tables, sinksEvery shift or as required by your health code
Floors, floor drains, and matsDaily
Fryers, grills and flat-tops (detail clean)Daily to weekly, based on use
Walk-in coolers and shelvingWeekly to monthly
Behind and under equipmentWeekly to monthly
Walls, ceilings, light fixturesMonthly to quarterly
Exhaust hood filtersWeekly to monthly, depending on volume
Hood, plenum and duct systemPer NFPA 96, see below

These are planning guidelines. Your local health inspector and your equipment manuals have the final say.

Exhaust hood and duct cleaning

NFPA 96, the fire-safety standard for commercial cooking ventilation, sets inspection and cleaning intervals based on the type and volume of cooking. In general:

  • Monthly for systems serving solid-fuel cooking
  • Quarterly for high-volume cooking such as 24-hour operations, charbroiling or wok cooking
  • Semiannually for moderate-volume cooking
  • Annually for low-volume operations, such as day camps or churches

Your insurer and fire marshal may also have requirements. Hood and duct cleaning should be done by a trained, insured contractor who gives you a service sticker and a report.

Signs you are overdue

  • Grease on walls, ceilings or the hood edge
  • Persistent odors even after cleaning
  • Pests near the kitchen
  • Slippery floors that stay slick after mopping

Who should do it

Routine kitchen cleaning is often done by kitchen staff or a cleaning vendor. Heavy deep cleaning and hood work are specialized. Ask any vendor whether kitchen deep cleaning is in their regular scope or something they subcontract.
