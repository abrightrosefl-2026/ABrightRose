Frequently Asked Questions

What hospitality operators and venue managers typically want to know before booking.

Can you clean between events with short turnaround times?

Yes, event space turnover cleaning is a core part of our hospitality service — we scope turnaround windows to your event calendar so spaces are guest-ready between bookings.

Do you offer cleaning outside normal business hours?

Yes, we schedule around your peak hours, events, and guest flow, including early morning, overnight, or between-shift service windows.

What's the difference between your hospitality and daycare service listed together?

We now offer dedicated programs for each — hospitality facilities (hotels, event venues, restaurants) and childcare facilities have different product, scheduling, and compliance needs, so we scope and quote them separately even though both prioritize guest or child safety.

Do you provide fogging or disinfection for guest areas?

Yes, electrostatic fogging and enhanced disinfection are available for guest-facing areas, commonly used seasonally or after high-traffic events.

How much does hospitality facility cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on square footage, guest traffic, and event frequency. We provide a custom, predictable quote after a free walkthrough of your venue.

Can you handle recurring event-based contracts, not just nightly cleaning?

Yes, many of our hospitality clients combine a base recurring schedule with event-triggered turnover cleaning — we'll build a plan that matches how your venue actually operates.

Are you insured to work in guest-facing commercial spaces?

Yes, ABR is fully insured and can provide certificates of insurance as required by your venue, landlord, or event contracts.
