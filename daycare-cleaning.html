Frequently Asked Questions

What daycare directors and owners typically ask before choosing a cleaning partner.

What cleaning products are safe to use around children?

We use Green Seal-certified and EPA Safer Choice products, which are formulated to be non-toxic and low-odor. For surfaces children have direct contact with — toys, tables, nap mats — we follow rinse or dry-time guidance so nothing is left behind that could be ingested or cause skin irritation.

How often should a daycare be professionally cleaned?

Most licensed daycares are cleaned nightly, five to seven days a week, with high-touch disinfection layered in daily by classroom staff between ABR visits. We'll scope frequency to your enrollment size and state licensing requirements.

Do you disinfect toys and play equipment?

Yes, toy and play-surface disinfection is a standard part of our daycare cleaning checklist, using products safe for the surfaces and age groups in your facility.

Is your staff background-checked before working in a childcare facility?

Yes, ABR crew members are screened before assignment, and we're glad to provide documentation your facility needs to satisfy licensing or parent-handbook requirements.

Can you clean during an illness outbreak at our daycare?

Yes — electrostatic fogging and an intensified disinfection pass are available on short notice when your facility is dealing with a stomach bug, flu, or other outbreak, in addition to your regular schedule.

Do you clean diaper-changing stations?

Yes, diaper-changing station sanitation is included and follows the same disinfection protocol expected by most state health department licensing standards.

How much does daycare cleaning cost?

Cost depends on square footage, number of classrooms, and visit frequency. We provide a custom quote after a free walkthrough — daycares vary widely in layout, so we don't quote sight-unseen.
