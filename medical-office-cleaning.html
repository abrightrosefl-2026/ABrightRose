Frequently Asked Questions

The questions we hear most from office managers and practice administrators evaluating a medical cleaning partner.

How often should a medical office be professionally cleaned?

Most medical and dental offices are cleaned nightly on weeknights, with high-touch surfaces disinfected between patient visits by in-office staff. ABR builds a schedule around your patient volume and hours — many practices add a deeper weekly service for floors and restrooms on top of nightly cleaning.

What cleaning products are safe to use in a medical office?

We use Green Seal-certified and EPA Safer Choice products for general cleaning, paired with EPA-registered disinfectants for high-touch and treatment areas. This combination meets infection-control expectations without the harsh fumes or residue that can bother patients and staff with sensitivities.

Is your cleaning staff trained for healthcare environments?

Yes. Every ABR crew member completes our disinfection and infection control training module before working in a medical setting, covering high-touch surface mapping, correct contact times, and cross-contamination prevention with color-coded microfiber systems.

Do you offer electrostatic fogging for medical offices?

Yes, electrostatic fogging is available as an add-on service, commonly used during flu season, after a confirmed illness in the office, or as a periodic deep-sanitation measure alongside your regular cleaning schedule.

Can you clean around our office hours and patient schedule?

Yes — most medical office cleaning happens after hours or early morning, before patients arrive. We'll build your schedule around your specific hours so cleaning never conflicts with appointments.

Are you insured to work in a medical facility?

Yes, ABR is fully insured, and we're happy to provide certificates of insurance and any documentation your practice or property manager requires before service begins.

How much does medical office cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on square footage, visit frequency, and the specific scope (exam rooms, restrooms, common areas). We provide a customized, predictable quote after a free walkthrough of your space — there's no one-size-fits-all rate for healthcare facilities.
