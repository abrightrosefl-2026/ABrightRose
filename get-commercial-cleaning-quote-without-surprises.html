How to Get a Commercial Cleaning Quote Without Surprises

Published by the ABR team · Updated October 2026

Key takeaways
  • Insist on a walkthrough and an itemized, written quote.
  • Ask what is not included, what could raise the price and what supplies cost.
  • Confirm the first invoice matches the quote before you commit long term.
Published by A Bright Rose Cleaning Services, a commercial-only cleaning company in West Palm Beach, FL serving Palm Beach County.

Surprises usually come from unspoken assumptions. The fix is to say everything out loud and put it in writing.

Step 1: Prepare your information

  • Address and total square footage
  • Number of restrooms and break rooms
  • Floor types (carpet, tile, vinyl, hard floor)
  • Hours you want service and how access works
  • Special needs, such as medical or daycare disinfection requirements

Step 2: Require a walkthrough

A quote without a walkthrough is a guess. A good company will measure, ask questions and note details such as clutter, glass and floor condition.

Step 3: Ask for an itemized written quote

It should show:

  • Service days and hours
  • Tasks and their frequency
  • Monthly price
  • What is included for supplies (paper, soap, liners)
  • Exclusions
  • Add-on pricing for extras
  • Contract term and notice to cancel
  • How and when prices can change

Step 4: Ask the "what if" questions

  • What if we add a restroom or an extra day?
  • What if we need a holiday or emergency visit?
  • What if supply prices change?
  • What is the charge for a re-clean if something is missed?
  • Is there any setup or startup fee?

Step 5: Verify before you sign

Check the insurance, references and contract terms. See how to verify credentials.

Step 6: Review the first invoice

Compare the first invoice to the quote. If anything differs, ask right away, in writing.

Rule: If a cost is not in the quote, you should not be surprised by it later. Ask the question now.
