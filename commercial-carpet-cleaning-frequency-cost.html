Commercial Carpet Cleaning: How Often and How Much?

Published by the ABR team · Updated October 2026

Key takeaways
  • Daily vacuuming does most of the work. Deep cleaning is typically every 6 to 12 months, and more often in high-traffic areas.
  • Methods include hot water extraction and low-moisture options. Choose based on carpet type, traffic and downtime.
  • Cost depends on area, soil level, access and method. Ask for pricing by square foot and a minimum.
Published by A Bright Rose Cleaning Services, a commercial-only cleaning company in West Palm Beach, FL serving Palm Beach County.

Carpet is a major investment, and dirt is what wears it out. Regular maintenance extends its life.

How often

  • Vacuum: daily in traffic areas, at least a few times a week elsewhere. Soil removal is the most important thing you can do.
  • Spot treatment: as soon as spills happen.
  • Interim cleaning: every few months in busy entrances and corridors, using low-moisture methods.
  • Deep cleaning (extraction): most manufacturers recommend about every 6 to 12 months for commercial carpet, and more often for heavy traffic. Always check your carpet manufacturer's warranty instructions.

Common methods

MethodHow it worksGood for
Hot water extractionSprays hot water and solution, then extracts itDeep cleaning, heavy soil
Low-moisture or encapsulationUses minimal water and a polymer that traps soilFrequent maintenance, quick drying
Bonnet cleaningRotary machine with absorbent padSurface maintenance
Dry compoundAbsorbent compound brushed in and vacuumedLittle downtime

Drying time matters. Plan cleaning when the space is closed or lightly used, and make sure the area is ventilated. In humid South Florida, slow drying can lead to odors if too much water is used.

What affects cost

  • Total area and how the space is broken up
  • Soil level and stains
  • Method chosen
  • Furniture moving and access
  • After-hours scheduling
  • Minimum charges for small jobs

Ask for the price by square foot, plus any minimum, and whether furniture moving and spot treatment are included. We are not quoting a number here, because rates depend on your market and scope.

Protect carpet between cleanings

  • Use walk-off mats at entrances
  • Vacuum with a commercial machine and good filtration
  • Treat spills quickly, and blot rather than rub
  • Keep a schedule for high-traffic zones
  • Keep records of cleaning for warranty purposes

Choosing a provider

Ask about the method, drying time, certifications and insurance. The Carpet and Rug Institute publishes guidance and a Seal of Approval program for products and equipment.

Tip: Test a small area first if you are unsure about colorfastness or results.
