Frequently Asked Questions

What pastors, administrators and facility committees typically ask before choosing a cleaning partner.

What areas of a house of worship do you clean?

Sanctuaries and pews, fellowship halls, classrooms, nurseries, restrooms, kitchens, offices, entrances and parking-lot walkways. We build the checklist around your building and the way your congregation uses it.

Can you clean around our service and event schedule?

Yes. We schedule around services, classes and events, usually on weeknights, early mornings or between gatherings, and we can add extra visits before and after weddings, funerals and holidays.

Are your products safe for wood pews, carpet and finishes?

We use Green Seal and EPA Safer Choice products for general cleaning and match the product to the surface, such as finished wood, carpet and stone. Tell us about special finishes or sacred items during the walkthrough and we will mark them as hands-off or special care.

Do you clean nurseries and children's classrooms?

Yes. Nursery and classroom cleaning uses child-safe, non-toxic products, with toys, tables and high-touch surfaces disinfected according to the product label and contact time.

Do you clean kitchens and fellowship halls?

Yes. We clean and sanitize kitchen surfaces, tables, chairs and floors after events. Equipment deep-cleaning and grease-hood cleaning are specialty tasks we can scope separately.

Do you serve all faiths?

Yes. We clean churches, synagogues, mosques, temples and other places of worship, and we follow any house rules you give us, such as shoe policies, restricted rooms and items that must not be moved.

How much does worship facility cleaning cost?

Cost depends on square footage, number of rooms, how often you meet and how often you want cleaning. We provide a free walkthrough and a written, itemized quote, and we do not quote sight unseen.
