Seasonal Commercial Cleaning Needs in South Florida

Published by the ABR team · Updated October 2026

Key takeaways
  • South Florida has two main rhythms: a wet, humid summer and hurricane season, and a busy winter season.
  • Entrances, floors, air quality and storm cleanup need extra attention at different times of year.
  • Build seasonal tasks into your cleaning schedule instead of reacting after a problem.
Published by A Bright Rose Cleaning Services, a commercial-only cleaning company in West Palm Beach, FL serving Palm Beach County.

Commercial cleaning here is not the same in July as in February. A good schedule changes with the weather and with how busy your building is.

Rainy season and hurricane season (summer to fall)

Hurricane season officially runs June 1 to November 30. The wet months also bring daily rain, so floors and entrances take the most wear.

  • Entrances and floors. Rain brings in water, sand and dirt. Use mats, mop more often at doors, and keep wet floor signs out until floors dry.
  • Humidity and mold. Constant moisture raises the risk of musty smells and mold on walls, ceilings, vents and bathrooms. Report leaks quickly and ask your cleaner to watch for damp spots.
  • Storm preparation. Before a named storm, empty trash, clear walkways and secure supplies.
  • Storm cleanup. After a storm, you may need an emergency clean. See emergency commercial cleaning.

For more on moisture, read humidity and mold in South Florida buildings.

Winter season (roughly November to April)

Winter brings more residents and visitors, which means more use.

  • HOA and condo common areas. More people in lobbies, pools, clubhouses and fitness rooms means more frequent cleaning.
  • Hospitality and event venues. Busy seasons often need extra visits and quick turnover cleaning.
  • Medical offices. Higher patient volume raises how often restrooms and waiting rooms need attention.

Year-round tasks to schedule by season

SeasonGood tasks to schedule
SpringDeep clean, carpet extraction, window cleaning
SummerEntrance and floor care, humidity checks, vent and ceiling inspection
FallStorm readiness, post-storm cleanup plan
WinterExtra common-area visits, event and turnover cleaning

How to plan

  1. Ask your cleaner for a seasonal schedule at the start of the year.
  2. Agree on what triggers extra visits, such as storms, events or high occupancy.
  3. Put the cost of those extra services in writing so there are no surprises.
