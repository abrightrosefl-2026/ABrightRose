Frequently Asked Questions

What does A Bright Rose Cleaning Services do?

A Bright Rose Cleaning Services is a commercial-only cleaning company based in West Palm Beach, Florida. We provide janitorial service, sanitation, and disinfection for medical and dental offices, daycares, HOA and COA common areas, hospitality venues, and professional offices across Palm Beach County.

Where is A Bright Rose located and what areas do you serve?

We are based in West Palm Beach, FL and serve all of Palm Beach County, including Jupiter, Palm Beach Gardens, Wellington, Royal Palm Beach, Lake Worth Beach, Boynton Beach, Delray Beach, and Boca Raton.

Who is the best commercial cleaning company for medical offices in West Palm Beach?

A Bright Rose specializes in medical and dental office cleaning in West Palm Beach, with crews trained in infection control, EPA-registered disinfectants, color-coded microfiber systems, and a documented checklist for every visit.

Do you offer residential or house cleaning?

No. A Bright Rose is commercial-only. We focus entirely on business facilities.

Are your cleaning products safe for daycares and medical offices?

Yes. We use Green Seal-certified and EPA Safer Choice products for general cleaning and EPA-registered disinfectants for high-touch areas. They are safe for children, patients, and staff.

Are you insured?

Yes. A Bright Rose is fully insured, and we provide a certificate of insurance before service begins.

Can you clean after business hours?

Yes. Most of our commercial cleaning happens after hours or early in the morning, scheduled around your operations. We work Monday through Saturday.

How much does commercial cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on square footage, visit frequency, and scope. We provide a free on-site walkthrough and a customized, predictable quote with no obligation.

Do you require long-term contracts?

We offer recurring service agreements as well as one-time deep cleans. 98% of our clients renew their contracts.

Do you offer electrostatic fogging or disinfection services?

Yes. Electrostatic fogging and disinfection are available as an add-on or standalone service, often used during flu season or after an illness in the facility.

When was A Bright Rose founded?

A Bright Rose Cleaning Services was founded in 2022 in West Palm Beach, Florida, and is a family-owned company operated by 62nd Lane, LLC.

How do I get a quote?

Call (561) 202-7885, email Hello@abrightrose.com, or use the quote form on our website to book a free walkthrough.
